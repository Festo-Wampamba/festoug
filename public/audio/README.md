# Assistant engine audio

Active asset: `engine-idle-loop.mp3`
Source: user-supplied `freesound_community-starting-suburban-26127.mp3`.
The stable running section at 3–10 seconds was selected after comparing the
level envelopes of all three supplied recordings. Starter and shutdown sections
were excluded. A 0.5-second tail/head crossfade creates a 6.5-second loop, with
45 Hz high-pass / 4.5 kHz low-pass filtering and loudness normalization. Export:
44.1 kHz, 128 kbps MP3. The source's rights remain those of its original owner;
no new license claim is made here.

Sound defaults off. Once enabled it idles continuously, revs during scrolling,
returns smoothly to idle afterward, and mutes while the tab is hidden.
